# IPA Keyboard — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 11, 2026**

IPA Keyboard is an iOS application and custom keyboard extension for entering International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) symbols and related phonetic characters.

This policy describes how IPA Keyboard handles information when you use the app and its keyboard extension.

## 1. Text input and data collection

IPA Keyboard does **not** collect, record, transmit, or upload the text you type or the surrounding text in other apps.

IPA Keyboard does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The app and keyboard do not send your typed content to the developer or to external servers.

Text you enter into other applications is handled by those applications under their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information stored on your device

IPA Keyboard saves the settings needed to provide its features locally on your device. These may include:

- Interface language and keyboard appearance preferences.
- Visible symbol categories and panel settings.
- Custom swipe and input preferences.
- Optional sound and haptic feedback preferences.
- Recently used symbols.

The main app and keyboard extension share relevant settings using Apple's App Group storage. This information is used to operate the keyboard and is not uploaded to the developer.

## 3. Full Access permission

Basic character input works without enabling **Allow Full Access**.

Some optional functions, such as keyboard click sounds, may depend on Full Access under iOS restrictions. Enabling Full Access grants a third-party keyboard additional system capabilities, but IPA Keyboard does not use that permission to collect or transmit typed text or surrounding content.

You can manage the keyboard and its permissions in iOS Settings.

## 4. Network connections and third parties

IPA Keyboard's core keyboard and symbol-reference functions work offline.

The app does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not sell or share your typing data with third parties.

If you follow a link to GitHub or another external website, that website's own privacy policy will apply.

## 5. Your choices and data deletion

You can change your keyboard preferences in the app and remove IPA Keyboard from your enabled keyboards in iOS Settings.

Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored application data from the device. Copies in device backups, if any, are managed according to iOS and your backup settings.

## 6. Support communications

If you voluntarily contact the developer using GitHub, the content you submit is processed by GitHub under its own terms and privacy policy. GitHub issues may be publicly visible. Please do not include passwords, private text, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be available at this URL, with an updated effective date.
